Executive Summary & Business Problem

In high-end hospitality operations, bar managers face two conflicting operational bottlenecks: Stockouts of high-demand spirits during peak weekend rushes (damaging guest satisfaction, losing revenue, and interrupting service) versus overstocking slow-moving items (tying up working capital, consuming scarce backroom storage, and increasing shrinkage/spoilage risks).

Krystal Ball converts transaction bottle balance logs into continuous daily bar-brand consumption time series, enforces physical inventory conservation laws, applies machine learning demand forecasting, and dynamically calculates lead-time safety stock buffers ($L=2\text{ days}$) to optimize working capital while eliminating service disruptions.
